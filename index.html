About the seminar

The Finance Seminar brings leading researchers in finance and macro-finance to the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta to present current work, on selected Thursdays during the academic year. A small number of seats are held for external academic visitors, who can reserve online and receive a confirmation and a reminder.
