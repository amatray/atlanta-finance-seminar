What a visit includes

The seminarA research talk at the Bank

Economists from around the world present current work in finance and macro-finance, 12:00 to 1:15 PM Eastern at the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta in Midtown.

Day visitSpend the day in the department

Visitors can arrange to spend the day in the Research Department around the seminar and meet with economists working in related areas. Write to the organizers when you reserve a seat.

ParkingOnsite parking

Parking at the Atlanta Fed is available for registered visitors.

EntryStraightforward entry

Bring a government-issued photo ID and arrive a few minutes early for security screening. Detailed entry instructions come with your seat confirmation.

About the seminar

The Finance Seminar is one of the seminar series run by the Research Department of the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta, covering topics in finance and macro-finance. Economists from around the world are invited to present, and seminars are open to academic researchers associated with local universities and research institutions. Researchers who wish to attend register at least 72 hours in advance; this site provides that sign-up for external academic visitors. The official seminar listing and the full department program are maintained on the Atlanta Fed website.

Planning to attend?

Seats are limited and open to external academic visitors. Reserve one from the schedule, or write with any question about a session.
