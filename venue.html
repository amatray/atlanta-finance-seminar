Entering the Bank

The Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta is a secure building. Visitors must present a government-issued photo ID and clear a security screening on entry, so please arrive a few minutes early. Parking is available. Detailed entry instructions are included with your seat confirmation.

Planning to attend?

Seats are limited and open to external academic visitors. Reserve one from the schedule, or write with any question about a session.
